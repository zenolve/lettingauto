14B. Renters' Rights Act 2025 — Additional Obligations

14B.1 Pets. From 1 May 2026, tenants have a statutory right under Section 11 of the Renters' Rights Act 2025 to request consent to keep a pet at the Property. The Landlord must not unreasonably refuse such a request and must respond in writing within 28 days (or within 7 days of any further information requested). Refusal is only permitted on reasonable grounds. The Landlord may require the Tenant to obtain pet damage insurance as a condition of consent. The Landlord must notify Palace Gate promptly upon receipt of any pet request so that it can be handled in compliance with the Act.

14B.2 Rent in Advance. From 1 May 2026, it is a civil offence under the Renters' Rights Act 2025 for a landlord or their agent to request or accept more than one month's rent in advance for an assured tenancy in England. The Landlord confirms that neither they nor Palace Gate on their behalf will request or accept rent in advance exceeding one month. Breach may result in a local authority civil penalty of up to £5,000. This restriction does not apply to non-Housing Act tenancies.

14B.3 Anti-Discrimination. From 1 May 2026, it is unlawful under the Renters' Rights Act 2025 for a landlord or agent to refuse to let, or to apply discriminatory criteria when advertising or letting a property, on the grounds that a prospective tenant is in receipt of housing benefit or other benefits, or has children. The Landlord confirms they will not instruct Palace Gate to apply any such criteria. An initial breach may result in a civil penalty of up to £7,000; repeat breaches up to £40,000.

14B.4 Rent Increases. From 1 May 2026, rent increases for assured tenancies in England may only be made once per year, using the statutory Section 13 procedure under the Housing Act 1988 (Form 4A from 1 May 2026), with a minimum of two months' written notice to the Tenant. Any contractual rent review clause purporting to allow increases more frequently or by any other mechanism will cease to have effect from that date. Tenants have the right to challenge any proposed increase at the First-tier Tribunal. The Landlord authorises Palace Gate to serve Section 13 notices on their behalf when instructed to do so.

14B.5 Student Possession (Ground 4A). Where the Property is let to full-time students, the Landlord may wish to rely on Ground 4A of the Housing Act 1988 (as amended by the Renters' Rights Act 2025) to recover possession at the end of the academic year. To preserve the right to use this ground, written notice of the intention to use it must be served on the Tenant by 31 May 2026 in most cases. This Information Sheet does not constitute that notice. Palace Gate can advise and serve the relevant notice on the Landlord's behalf if instructed.
