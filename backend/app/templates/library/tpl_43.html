Visa requirements (Right to Rent): It is a legal requirement that all landlords check that tenants renting a property in England have the right to rent in the UK. British and Irish citizens have an automatic right to rent; everyone else must provide evidence of their immigration status (for example a Home Office share code or a valid visa) covering the proposed tenancy. Should the tenancy continue beyond the validity of your permission to stay, the Landlord will need to carry out a follow-up check.

  4. An inventory of the items and condition of the property will be checked with you on the tenancy start date. It is important that you are present for the handover.
