1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 “Landlord” means anyone owning an interest in the Property, whether freehold or leasehold, entitling them to possession of it upon the termination or expiry of the Tenancy and anyone who subsequently owns the Property. It may also include anyone acting as the Landlord`s agent, Agent, Rental collection agent, or managing agent.

1.2 “Tenant” means anyone entitled to possession of the Property under this Agreement and includes the successors in title to the Tenant. Where the Tenant is more than one person all obligations and agreements on the part of the Tenant herein contained shall be deemed to have been undertaken jointly and severally. The Landlord may seek to enforce these obligations and claim damages against any one or more of those individuals.

Where the Tenant is more than one person, the Tenant’s covenants are joint and several. The expression “joint and several” means that jointly the persons forming the Tenant are responsible for the payment of all Rent and all liabilities falling upon the Tenant during the Tenancy or any extension thereof as well as any breach of the Agreement; and individually each Tenant is responsible for payment of all Rent and all liabilities falling upon the persons forming the Tenant as well as any breach of the Agreement, until all debts have been discharged in full.

1.3 “Occupier” means any person who is licensed by the Landlord to reside at the Property and who will be bound by all the terms of this Agreement, apart from the payment of Rent.

1.4 The “Agent” means Palace Gate of 11 Palace Gate, London W8 5LS.

1.5 “Guarantor” means the person responsible for discharging the Tenant’s obligations if the Tenant defaults, whether the Landlord elects to pursue the Tenant or not.

1.6 “Property” means any part or parts of the building boundaries fences garden and outbuildings belonging to the Landlord unless they have been specifically excluded from the Tenancy. When the Tenancy is part of a larger building the Property includes the use of common access ways and facilities.

1.7 “Fixtures & Fittings” means references to any of the fixtures, fittings, furnishings, or effects, floor, ceiling or wall coverings as per the Inventory agreed between the parties.

1.8 “Inventory & Schedule of Condition” means the document drawn up prior to the commencement of the Tenancy by the Landlord or Inventory Clerk which shall include the Fixtures & Fittings in the Property as defined above, a copy of which shall be signed by the Parties. The Landlord shall pay for the Inventory, Schedule of Condition and check-In report. At the termination of the Tenancy an Inventory Clerk will do a check-out against the Inventory which will be payable by the Tenant.

1.9 “Term” or “Tenancy” (set out in Clause 3 of this Agreement) means the fixed term and any extension or continuation of the contractual Tenancy or any periodic Tenancy arising after the expiry of the original Term.

1.10 "Deposit" means the money held by the Agent (in a Stakeholder capacity) during the Tenancy as security against any damage to the Property or other breaches of the Tenancy Agreement.

1.11 “Stakeholder” means that the Deposit is held by Palace Gate as Stakeholder. At the end of the Tenancy, no deductions can be made from the Deposit by the Stakeholder without the consent in writing of both the Landlord and the Tenant, or in the event of a dispute, the consent of the court or an adjudication decision from TDS.

1.12 “TDS” means the Tenancy Deposit Scheme of which Palace Gate is a Member.

1.13 “ICE” means the Independent Case Examiner of The Dispute Service (TDS).

1.14 “Member” means the Agent who is a member of the Tenancy Deposit Scheme.

1.15 "Notice Period" means the period of notice that the Landlord and the Tenant must give to each other.

1.16 "Emergency" means where there is a risk to life or damage to the fabric of the Property or Fixtures & Fittings contained therein.

1.17 “Water rates” means any references to water sewerage and environmental service charges.

1.18 “Joint and several” means when more than one person comprise the Tenant, they will each be responsible for complying with the Tenant’s obligations under this Agreement both individually and together. The Landlord may seek to enforce these obligations and claim damages against any one or more of those individuals.

1.19 References to the singular include the plural and references to the masculine include the feminine.

1.20 References to persons include firms companies and corporations.

1.21 The Landlord and the Tenant agree that this Agreement will be subject to the jurisdiction of the Court in England and Wales and that the laws of England and Wales shall apply to this Agreement.
