FAQ

Questions, before you ask.

Do we have to migrate our existing data?
Lorents imports from Airtable, spreadsheets, or any CSV in one step. Our onboarding team maps your fields to the standard tenancy model and keeps a backup of every original record.
Which e-signing provider do you use?
DocuSign by default. If you already use Adobe Sign, DocuSeal or signNow we can plug into it — most connectors are off-the-shelf.
Where is our data hosted?
UK-region cloud, ISO 27001 certified. Daily backups, point-in-time recovery, encrypted at rest and in transit. GDPR by default.
Can we white-label the emails landlords & tenants see?
Yes. Logo, colours, sending domain, footer copy — everything the landlord and tenant see is your brand, not ours.
What happens when a law changes?
Compliance logic is part of the platform — we ship updates the moment legislation lands. The RRA 2025 reached every customer the week it received royal assent.
