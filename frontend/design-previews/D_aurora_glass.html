Why LettingAuto

The hard parts of lettings, handled.

Everything between a landlord instruction and a settled tenant lives in one pipeline — checked, signed and reconciled without the chasing.

Compliance gates, not checklists

Gas safety, EPC, EICR, How to Rent and deposit protection are verified at the gate. If anything is missing or expired, the move-in simply cannot proceed — aligned with the Renters’ Rights Act 2025 from day one.
